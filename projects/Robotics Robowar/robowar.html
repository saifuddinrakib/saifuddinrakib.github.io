🤖 Robowar - Combat Robot

Overview

This project was developed for the Mecceleration IUT 18 robotics competition, where we participated in the Robowar category. The challenge was to design and build a combat robot capable of anchoring and defeating opponent robots in an arena battle.

Our team designed a heavy-duty robot with a dual-mechanism system: a mechanical hand-like anchor to grip and control opponent robots, and a high-speed rotating weapon to deliver powerful strikes. The design focused on structural strength, mechanical efficiency, and strategic combat capabilities.

Awards

Technical Components

  • Anchor Mechanism: A hand-like gripping system designed to capture and hold opponent robots in place
  • Rotating Weapon: High-speed rotating blade system for delivering powerful strikes to anchored opponents
  • Heavy Structure: Robust chassis built to withstand impacts and maintain stability during combat
  • Drive System: Powerful motors for maneuverability and positioning in the arena
  • Control System: Wireless remote control for precise operation of both anchor and weapon mechanisms
